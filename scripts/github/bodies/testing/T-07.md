## Summary

Browser E2E for the player pages — Discover, company and pitch detail — with screenshots.

## Scope

- Discover: list, filters update URL and results, debounced search (one request), sort, reset, empty state, city dialog (diacritic search), first visit without a city, phone-width drawer
- Map view: shows the "needs a key" message without a key; with `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` set, pills render and a click opens the preview (skipped when no key)
- Company page: hero, hours accordion, pitches, reviews tab with paging and pitch filter, 404
- Pitch page: gallery + lightbox (thumbnails, arrows, Esc), amenities, pricing, 7-day availability dots matching fixtures, closed-day message, Book Now signed in / signed out → login → back, restricted and inactive states, wrong-company redirect
- Desktop 1280px and 375px
