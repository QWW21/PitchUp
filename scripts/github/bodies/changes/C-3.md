## Change

Pitch size (5v5, 6v6, 7v7, 11v11) stays, but it is the **owner's recommended number of players per team**, not a limit: the owner is paid the same however many people come. 6v6 is the most common format and is added.

## Scope

- Schema and shared: `SIX_A_SIDE` ("6v6"), filterable on Discover
- `SIZE_INFO` text: "The number of players per team the owner recommends for the best game. It is not a limit: the price is the same however many of you come."
- Web: an (i) button beside the size on the pitch page and on the "Pitch size" filter; the text appears on hover and on keyboard focus, and is the button's accessible description
- Mobile: the same (i) on the pitch page and in the filters sheet; a tap shows the text (phones have no hover)
- Seed: two Cluj pitches are 6v6

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hovering or focusing the (i) shows the text; a screen reader reads it
- [ ] `size=SIX_A_SIDE` filters Discover
